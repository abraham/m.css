Cards

Every article is a card. A container holding only articles lays them out as a grid of equal-height cards.

Accessibility

An article is a self-contained piece of content. For a long-form page, a lone article in main stays plain text instead of a card.

Customize

Cards use the overlay surface and 1.5 times --m-radius.

Tables

Tables get row rules, a sticky header row, and tabular numbers.

Accessibility

Give header cells a scope, and the table a caption.

Customize

--m-density sets the cell padding.

Media

Images and video scale down to the column and keep their aspect ratio.

Accessibility

Describe images with alt. Set width and height to avoid layout shift.

Customize

Images keep the page's corner radius.

Details

A details element opens and closes smoothly where the browser supports it.

Accessibility

The summary is a button to screen readers, and opening happens instantly when motion is reduced.

Customize

The open animation follows the motion tokens.
