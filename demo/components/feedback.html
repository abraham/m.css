Progress

A progress with a value shows how far along a task is. Without a value, it shows a sliding bar.

Accessibility

Label the bar. The indeterminate bar pulses instead of sliding when motion is reduced.

Customize

The fill uses --m-primary.

Meter

A meter shows a measurement within a known range, such as disk usage.

Accessibility

Put the value in the element's text too, as a fallback.

Customize

Meters keep the browser's own colors for low, high, and optimum ranges.

Busy

aria-busy="true" on any element shows an inline spinner while its content loads.

Accessibility

Screen readers wait until aria-busy is removed before announcing the region's changes.

Customize

The spinner follows the text color, and pulses instead of spinning when motion is reduced.
