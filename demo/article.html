Navigating between pages

This second page exists to exercise cross-document view transitions. In browsers that support @view-transition, following a link between the two demo pages cross-fades instead of flashing. Other browsers navigate normally.

The transition uses the slow duration token, so it turns off with reduced motion, either from the system setting or from data-motion="reduce".
