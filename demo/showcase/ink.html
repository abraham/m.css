Honeyford Community Association

Spring Fayre

21 April, 11am to 3pm, at Mill Beach Park. Free entry, and everyone is welcome.

Lend a hand

What's on

Music is at the bandstand, and everything is free, apart from tombola tickets at £1 a strip
Activity When
Tombola All day
Cakes and drinks All day
Samba band Noon
Dhol band 1:30pm
Craft stalls All day
Circus skills workshop 2pm

Getting there

Mill Beach Park
Promenade Road
Honeyford

The number 12 bus stops at the park gates. Parking is limited, so please walk or cycle if you can. Paths are step-free, and there are accessible toilets by the café.

Lend a hand

The fayre is run by neighbours. An hour of your time makes a real difference.

Good to know

What if it rains?

The fayre goes ahead in the marquee and the pavilion. The circus workshop moves indoors.

Can I bring my dog?

Yes, on a lead. There are water bowls by the café, and the beach is dog-friendly all day.

Do stalls take cards?

Most do, but bring some cash for the tombola and the cake stall.
